Size Guide

Find your perfect fit with our measurement charts. All sizes are in inches.

How to Measure

  • Chest: Measure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the tape level under your arms.
  • Shoulder: Measure from the edge of one shoulder to the other, across your back.
  • Sleeve: Measure from the shoulder edge down to your wrist.
  • Length: Measure from the base of your neck down to where you want the garment to end.
  • Waist: Measure around your natural waistline, where you normally wear trousers.

Tip: Use a soft measuring tape and measure over light clothing. If you are between two sizes, choose the larger one.
