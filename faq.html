Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about sizing, orders, shipping, and returns.

Sizing and Fit

How do I find my size?

Check our Size Guide and compare your chest, shoulder, and length measurements with the chart. If you are between two sizes, we recommend choosing the larger one.

Do you offer custom tailoring?

Yes, we can tailor selected items to your measurements. Please contact us with your details and we will let you know the price and delivery time.

Can I get alterations after receiving my order?

Minor alterations can be arranged through our team. Please contact us within 7 days of receiving your order.

Orders and Payment

How do I place an order?

Browse our collection, choose your item and size, and contact us through the Contact page to confirm your order.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash on delivery, bank transfer, and mobile wallet payments.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Orders can be changed or cancelled before they are dispatched. Contact us as soon as possible and we will help you.

Shipping and Delivery

How long does delivery take?

Ready-to-wear items usually arrive within 3 to 7 working days. Custom-tailored items take longer, and we will confirm the timeline when you order.

Do you deliver nationwide?

Yes, we deliver across the country. Shipping charges depend on your city and order size.

Returns and Exchanges

What is your return policy?

You can return unworn items in their original condition within 7 days of delivery. Custom-tailored items cannot be returned.

Can I exchange an item for a different size?

Yes, size exchanges are available within 7 days, subject to stock. Contact us to arrange it.

Care Instructions

How should I care for my outfit?

Kurtas and shalwar kameez can usually be washed gently or dry cleaned, depending on the fabric. Embroidered sherwanis and waistcoats should be dry cleaned only. Always check the care label on your item.

Still Have Questions?

We are happy to help. Visit our Contact page and we will get back to you as soon as possible.
